Lasagna

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Pasta sauce
  • Ricotta Cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Spread sauce in a baking dish and add a layer of lasagna sheets.
  2. Add ricotta and mozzarella.
  3. Repeat the layers and finish with mozzarella.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 30-40 minutes.
